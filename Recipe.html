Çiğ Köfte

Çiğ köfte is a popular Turkish dish traditionally made by kneading fine bulgur with tomato paste, spices, and (in its modern vegan version) walnuts instead of raw meat. It is soft, spicy, and tangy, and it is usually wrapped in a lettuce leaf with lemon juice and fresh parsley.

Ingredients

  • Fine bulgur
  • Tomato paste
  • Red pepper paste
  • Walnuts (or ground meat, in the traditional version)
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Lemon juice
  • Isot pepper and cumin
  • Fresh parsley and lettuce leaves, for serving

Instructions

  1. Soak the fine bulgur in warm water until it softens.
  2. Add the tomato paste and red pepper paste, and knead well.
  3. Finely chop the onion, garlic, and walnuts, and add them to the mixture.
  4. Season with isot pepper, cumin, and salt, then knead for at least 15 minutes until smooth.
  5. Add lemon juice and chopped parsley, and mix one more time.
  6. Shape into small ovals and serve wrapped in lettuce leaves with a wedge of lemon.
